About me

From a small town in Samba to Rashtrapati Bhavan.

I grew up in Smailpur, Samba (Jammu & Kashmir) and started in ethical hacking as a teenager. What began as curiosity turned into a mission: make ordinary people — children, women, and the officers who protect them — safe in a digital world moving faster than anyone can teach.

I founded Hackers Pathshala, Jammu & Kashmir's first cybersecurity company. Through it I've trained 5,000+ children across the country in cyber-safety and upskilled 100+ police officers at the Sher-i-Kashmir Police Academy in modern cybercrime investigation. My work has supported the Indian Army's Northern Command on cyber counter-intelligence, and I launched the nationwide Hack Free Bharat initiative.

In December 2024, President Droupadi Murmu conferred on me the Pradhan Mantri Rashtriya Bal Puraskar — India's highest civilian honour for children — in the Science & Technology category. Today I'm building thirdworld.io, the operating layer for AI agents, and Prarthana, which brings India's ancient scriptures to modern wellbeing.

About

From a self-taught hacker / infosec enthusiast to the President's office.

Not long ago, pulling the old hard drive out of a ThinkCentre workstation, I found my own beginnings in the files — JavaScript I'd written at 12, a Harvard CS course I was working through at 13. That jolt of nostalgia was also a realisation: I've only just started, and I can do far more.

What began as a kid's curiosity became a mission — make ordinary people, especially children and women, safe in a digital world moving faster than anyone can teach.

I founded Hackers Pathshala, Jammu & Kashmir's first cybersecurity company. Through it I've trained 5,000+ children across the country in cyber-safety and upskilled 100+ police officers in modern cybercrime investigation. My work has supported India's defense on cybersecurity, and I launched the nationwide Hack Free Bharat initiative.

In December 2024, President Droupadi Murmu conferred on me the Pradhan Mantri Rashtriya Bal Puraskar — India's highest honour for children — in Science & Technology. Today I'm building thirdworld.io, the operating layer for AI agents, and Prarthana, which brings India's ancient scriptures to modern wellbeing.
